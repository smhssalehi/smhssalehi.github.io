# Design versions

Version 1 is the initial approved visual direction: rounded research cards with left-hand thumbnails and the supplied portrait. A complete local source snapshot is preserved in `local/design-versions/version-1.zip` (ignored by Git and excluded from Jekyll). Extract it to a separate folder to compare or restore individual source files. No Git commit or push was made.

Version 2 changes research entries to an editorial list with fine rules, right-hand figures on larger screens, and one warm, left-accented Job Market Paper feature. Mobile entries prioritize paper titles and abstracts. The circular portrait is aligned to the top of its source image to provide more space above the head.
